Part 2 · Disarming Overage FOMU

The 5 Pricing Levers: What Drives Cost & How ESL Controls It

Buyers fear "black-box" usage meters where a traffic spike or an enthusiastic marketer accidentally triggers a $50,000 bill. Click any lever below or review the complete matrix to see how each metric is governed and how to explain it in plain, candid language.

LEVER 01 · ADMIN-GATED group

CMS Users & Environments

COMMITTED ALLOWANCE:
33 Users · 3 Environments · Content Graph Included

What drives it: Named admin/author seats inside SaaS CMS and provisioned environments (Dev / Test / Prod).

Why ESL can't accidentally over-spend: Seat and environment provisioning are 100% controlled by ESL's system administrators. Website visitors never consume a seat.

JOLT Comfort Frame:

"An author cannot accidentally create a 34th seat—your admins hold the keys."

LEVER 02 · PUBLISHING-GATED article

Published Content Items

COMMITTED ALLOWANCE:
2,250 Content Items + Built-in DAM

What drives it: Structured content items actively published and managed by ESL's marketing team.

Why ESL can't accidentally over-spend: Content volume only grows as fast as your own team authors pages. Reusing shared components and governed DAM assets across pages does not multiply your core item count uncontrollably.

JOLT Comfort Frame:

"Sized with headroom above your current footprint, and it only changes when your editors publish new content."

LEVER 03 · EXPERIMENTATION SCOPE science

Web Experimentation MAUs

COMMITTED ALLOWANCE:
250,000 Monthly Active Users (Enhanced + Personalization Essential)

What drives it: Unique monthly visitors who enter an active experiment or targeted experience.

Why ESL can't accidentally over-spend: ESL controls traffic allocation and page targeting. Running a test on the Personal Loans page only counts visitors to that page—not your entire site. And if overages ever occur, rates are pre-quoted in writing.

JOLT Comfort Frame:

"You control the throttle—you choose which pages run tests and what percentage of traffic enters an experiment."

LEVER 04 · AI CONSUMPTION bolt

Agent Platform Credits

COMMITTED ALLOWANCE:
100,000 Credits / Yr (Enhanced — Upgraded at $0 Extra Cost)

What drives it: Internal team usage of AI agents and workflows inside the platform.

Why ESL can't accidentally over-spend: Underlying LLM model costs sit inside the credits (no external token bills), and ESL has admin access controls and usage alerts (Slide 20) plus 2.5x the Essential credit pool at the exact same annual software price.

JOLT Comfort Frame:

"We upgraded you to 100,000 credits at no additional cost, and gave your admins alerts and role controls so AI usage never runs wild."

LEVER 05 · DELIVERY & API CALLS cloud_done

API Calls & Hosting Options

COMMITTED ALLOWANCE:
API Calls Included · Optional Front-End Hosting (+$39,600/yr for 5M visits)

What drives it: Content delivery requests via Content Graph and front-end page visits (if Optimizely hosts the front end).

Why ESL can't accidentally over-spend: API calls are included in the committed scope, and any usage above contracted allowances follows pre-negotiated, written overage rates—never punitive spot pricing.

JOLT Comfort Frame:

"API calls are included, and every future rate is locked in writing before you sign."

Cheat Sheet · In-the-Room Responses

3 Common "Overage & Lever" Questions — And How to Answer Them

QUESTION 01 · AI TOKEN ANXIETY

"What if our marketers use the AI agents heavily and blow past 100k credits?"

Sandler + JOLT Answer: "First, we bumped you from 40,000 (Essential) to 100,000 credits (Enhanced) at zero extra software cost so you have a 2.5x buffer. Second, model costs are included inside those credits, and your admins get usage alerts and role controls so you can throttle or reallocate before you ever hit 100%. And if you ever want more, extra credit packs are pre-priced in the written proposal."

QUESTION 02 · TRAFFIC SPIKE ANXIETY

"What if ESL runs a big member campaign and traffic spikes past 250,000 MAUs?"

Sandler + JOLT Answer: "Two protections: First, the 250,000 MAU allowance in Web Experimentation only counts visitors on pages where an experiment is actively running, and your team controls the traffic allocation percentage on every test. Second, API calls are included in the committed scope, and any usage above contracted allowances follows the written overage schedule—no surprise rate hikes."

QUESTION 03 · RENEWAL ANXIETY

"How do we know the price won't jump in Year 2, Year 3, or at renewal?"

Sandler + JOLT Answer: "Years 1, 2, and 3 are contractually fixed at $200,841.64/year for your committed scope—zero annual escalation. In fact, your total cash outlay drops by $46,000 after Year 1 once onboarding completes. And at the end of Month 36, any renewal adjustment requires 120 days' advance written notice."
