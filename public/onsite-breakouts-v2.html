[CREATIO PRIMER — FOR THE PRESENTER]

  • What it is: Creatio is a no-code CRM / workflow platform. Its Sales, Marketing and Service apps store the member relationship (contacts, accounts, opportunities, cases) and its visual designers model business processes without custom code. Creatio also markets a Financial Services offering aimed at banks and credit unions.
  • What it provides ESL: The system of record for relationship management: who the member is, which advisor/branch owns them, and which tasks or follow-ups are open.
  • How it connects: Creatio exposes open APIs (REST, OData, SOAP), which is how an OCP connector can read/write records between Creatio and ODP.
  • Positioning: ODP does not replace Creatio. Creatio keeps the relationship record; ODP adds real-time web behavior and audiences, and sends high-intent signals back to be acted on.
  • Confirm with ESL before the session: which Creatio apps they run, the exact objects/fields to sync, and whether a webhook receiver or API call will create the advisor task. The outbound step is a proposed design, not a built integration.

[FOCUS AREA #8: CRM — INFORMATION FLOW BETWEEN CDP + CRM]

  1. Inbound (Creatio CRM / Snowflake → ODP): Using Optimizely Connect Platform (OCP) REST webhooks or nightly Snowflake sync, Creatio pushes non-PII attributes (member_status, assigned_branch, prequalified_tier) into ODP.
  2. Consent Gate (#7): Every ODP event and identity stitch is conditional on CMP consent. Opted-out visitors generate no vuid profile and are never synced to Creatio.
  3. Zero-PII Identity Stitch: When a visitor signs in at /sign-in (esl_signed_in=1), ODP stitches their anonymous browsing behavior (/personal/loans calculator usage) to their known Member ID in under 50ms.
  4. Outbound (ODP → Creatio CRM): If an authenticated member interacts with the Personal Loan calculator and clicks "Start Application" without completing the FintechOS flow within 2 hours, ODP fires a real-time webhook back to Creatio CRM—routing a warm lead directly to their branch advisor.
